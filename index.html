How to use

Turn any photo into a movie shot.

  1. 1
    Drop a photo

    Drag, paste or pick one — or try a sample below. A small depth AI works out what's near and far.

  2. 2
    Pick a camera move

    Vertigo, Ken Burns, Orbit, Crane, Swing. Move your mouse (or tilt your phone) to peek around.

  3. 3
    Set the look

    Add haze, drag a light, click the scene to set focus, go miniature.

  4. 4
    Export a loop

    A seamless 4–6s video or a PNG still, ready for X.

Everything runs in your browser. Your photos are never uploaded.
